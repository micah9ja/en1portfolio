Haunted House

Bio

Dr.E told us to make groups, and assigned us to work on a project to be presented at a haunted house for Halloween in the Nolop Makerspace. Me, Michael, Jack, and Khang designed a surprise jack in the box, that shoots out a skeleton hand using the LEGO SPIKE Prime kit. We utilized scissor lifts to create the shooting out effect, and linked it to music and a scream playing as a person cranked the handle to initate it.

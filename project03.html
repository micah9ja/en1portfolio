Project 3: Biomimicry

Dr.E assigned us to take inspiration from nature and create a robot that can mimic its functions. When I saw the motors I immidiately thought of making an owl and having its head be able to rotrate 360 degrees. I then asked Claude AI to help me come up with or find designs, and this is what I came up with. It uses the battery pack to drive the main head motor, as well as the 2 medium motors used to mimic the owls feet, and also cling onto any ledge that it is propped on. Overall I am very satisfied with the result, and can't wait to see what I can come up with for the next project.

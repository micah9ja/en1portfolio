Project 4: Top Spinner

For this project I designed both a top and a top spinner mechanism. For the top I got an axle and stuck a wheel as a counterbalance on it, as well as a small 12 tooth gear to connect the top to the spinner. Then I connected the medium motor onto the hub using grey "picks", and attached both a large gear and a pivot containing a medium gear. This was so I could load the top and connect it to the medium gear without damaging it, and causing slipping issues.

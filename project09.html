Project 9: Robot Arm

Dr.E had us create a robotic arm using the Lego SPIKE Prime set, and our objective was to make it grab a ping pong ball and place it on a platform to form a chain. My final design had 3 degrees of motion, and had a claw setup to be able to grab the ping pong ball, which was detected by a color sensor. In the first round (which wasn't recorded) my robot was able to successfully grab the ping pong ball, but in the second attempt, which is shown in the video, it failed at grabbing it. Looking back I should have spent more time improving on the claw mechanism, but I still learnt a lot from the project!

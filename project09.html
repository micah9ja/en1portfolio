Personal Project 1

ESP32 PPG Heart-Rate Monitor

Bio

Developed a real-time biosignal monitor and wearable prototype using optical sensors and an ESP32 microcontroller to sample, filter, and process pulse data.

MAX30102 sensor integration: Processed noisy optical signals from pulsed infrared light to detect PPG heartbeats.

I2C multi-peripheral bus: Connected the MAX30102, MPU6050 accelerometer, and SSD1306 OLED to the ESP32 over GPIO pins.

High-speed firmware sampling: Programmed the ESP32 in C++ to sample biosignals at 250 Hz and estimate beats using a rolling average.

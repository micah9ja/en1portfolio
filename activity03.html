Activity 3: Paper Airplane

Assignment: Using Generative AI to design a paper airplane.

Dr.E gave us an assignment to get an AI to design a paper airplane, and then have it give us steps for how to fold it and make it fly. I used claude, and it gave me step by step instructions on how to make a basic airplane, and even gave clarifiying steps on when to unfold and how to fold the paper without having to be told to do so.

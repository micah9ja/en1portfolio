Project 8: Space Wrench

Dr.E had us make a prototype for a space wrench, to be used be astronauts. We had a lot of requirements that had to be fulfilled by the design. It had to be able to be operated with gloves, and in various positions such as upside down and vertical. Overall the design I chose was simple but was able to move very fast, although latching onto screws took a bit of time.

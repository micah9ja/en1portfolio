Project 12

Color and Height Lego Machine Learning

Bio

Dr.E this week taught us about machine learning and its applications. He taught us about nearest neighbour, k-nearest neighbor, and the different type of regressions used to take inputs and make them into outputs.

For this project we were assigned to show how we can feed our robot inputs such as height or color, and then have the robot make predictions about what we then give it after showing it some samples.

I used a nearest neighbor system where the heights and RGB values were stored in a list and then were used to compare against different colors and various heights.

I think this was the most interesting lesson I have learnt from Dr.E, and gave me a newfound appreciation for Computer Science!

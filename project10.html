Personal Project 2

Companion and Universal Badge

Bio

A custom, ESP32-powered wearable developer badge inspired by the GitHub Universe 2026 Badger, with its own mini operating system, apps, games, motion sensing, capacitive touch controls, infrared communication, and 3D-printed enclosure. Accompanying it will be a companion robot that is able to be played with, and also interact with the badge, similar to Skylanders.

  • Developed a preliminary 39-connection circuit and 3D breadboard layout, incorporating SPI/I²C communication, infrared transmission and reception, Wi-Fi/Bluetooth connectivity, ambient-light sensing, and 4 programmable LEDs.
  • Designing a compact 65–80 mm interactive robot companion using an ESP32-S3, 240 × 240 TFT display, 3-axis accelerometer, capacitive-touch sensor, RGB LED, and NFC tag for gesture-responsive animations and portfolio sharing.
  • Planning a modular 3D-printed enclosure featuring interchangeable faceplates, removable electronics, and wearable mounting options, emphasizing CAD, design for assembly, component integration, and repairability.
